Good to know
  • Some conversions use a large engine that downloads once and is then cached: video and audio ~30 MB, extra image formats ~15 MB, document formats ~60 MB, old Office files (.doc, .xls, .ppt) and full-layout PDFs ~250 MB. You're asked before anything big downloads.
  • PDF to Word works on text-based PDFs and rebuilds paragraphs and headings; tables and images are not preserved. Scanned PDFs need OCR, which isn't included.
  • Large videos can be slow, and the Office engine needs a few hundred MB of memory (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
